# Exact all-word realization of the fixed cubic pure-braid family

Date: 19 September 2026. Parent: `2dbac64674b39ae607ad6696f92f91b03ca3cbbd`.

## Result and its precise scope

An independent planar skein construction reproduces the complete archived
`H`, `H_A`, and `H_B` payload, with Git blob identity
`ed8d20c2c97d7cdcf62d4e87cae153e658d71224`. It uses no training coefficients.
The construction supplies the missing finite-dimensional realization for the
raw polynomial of the intended fixed cubic three-braid closure. Together with
the exact nonsingular Hankel block, this proves the all-word transfer law for
that specified diagram family. It is not an all-word proof for the separate
three commuting motif families, a proof that a voxel graph is a spatial spine,
or a complete classification of TPMS or Hamiltonian volumes.

A new geometry defect was found in the archived constructor during this check.
Its outer route control polygons have six unintended same-height intersections.
The standalone corrected constructor realizes the intended crossing-free
exterior without those intersections. The all-word theorem concerns this
explicit valid diagram family, not a claim that the old intersecting curves
were valid spatial embeddings. No old data or production file is overwritten.

## 1. Raw local relations

Write `q = Y + 2 + Y^(-1)` and `sigma=q-1`. For a crossing-free graph G,

    R(G) = sum_{S subset E(G)} (-1)^(|V(G)|+|S|) q^(|S|-|V(G)|+k(S)).

The empty graph has value 1; an isolated vertex has value -1. A circle has
value sigma, a cut edge gives zero, and a loop based at a vertex multiplies
the value of its deletion by -sigma. For a non-loop edge e,

    R(G)=R(G/e)+R(G-e).

The positive crossing used by the canonical braid constructor resolves as
`Y I + Y^(-1) E + V`, where I consists of straight strands, E of a cap and
cup, and V of the four-valent vertex. Its inverse exchanges Y and Y^(-1).
These are raw, fixed-diagram relations. Lowest-degree normalization is applied
after evaluation; it is not inserted into these linear relations.

## 2. The finite state space is constructed, not fitted

Cut the three braid strands at their left and right ends. Label the ports
0,1,2 and 3,4,5, with cyclic boundary order `(0,1,2,5,4,3)`.
Resolve the crossings, then eliminate internal ordinary edges by the local
relations. Closed components factor out. Suppress bivalent vertices; delete
loops with their exact factors. A component meeting only one port vanishes
under every closure because its attachment is a cut edge. The remaining
components are arcs or single stars joining at least two ports.

Planarity makes their boundary partition noncrossing. There are exactly 15
such partitions: five pairings, nine two-block partitions, and one six-port
block. The implementation enumerates them explicitly. Therefore every open
three-strand graph generated by the local operations is a linear combination
of these 15 states. This gives an upper bound, without assuming the rank of
an infinite Hankel matrix. It is consistent with Chbili's 15 spanning diagrams
for the three-strand Yamada module, but the implementation uses the raw local
relations above rather than importing a different normalization.

Each state is represented by a planar graph with marked ports. To stack two
states, identify matching intermediate ports and take the multiset union of
edges. Parallel arcs must remain separate. Eliminate the new internal graph
with the same relations. This defines all state products exactly over Z[q].
Let M_sigma1 and M_sigma2 be left multiplication by the two crossing diagrams.
Then

    M_A=M_sigma1^2,  M_B=M_sigma2^2.

For a word w, local skein linearity proves by induction that its tangle state
is M_w r, where r is the identity three-strand state. No word-length cutoff
enters this induction.

## 3. Fixed exterior and a valid spatial realization

The exterior has the same nine non-braid edges as the notebook: two middle
spacers, four side couplings, and three closing edges. With ports numbered as
above and the two remaining vertices numbered 6 and 7, its edge list is

    (1,6), (4,7), (0,1), (3,4), (6,2), (7,5), (3,0), (7,6), (5,2).

Close each of the 15 planar states with this exterior and evaluate its planar
polynomial by the subset formula. The resulting row is ell. Thus

    R_w = ell M_w r

for every word in A,B in the explicit fixed diagram family.

The archived outer routes assign (horizontal margin, roof height) equal to
(3,5), (2,4), (1,3) to top, middle and bottom. Their control polygons intersect
at `(-2,s,0)`, `(-1,s,0)`, `(-1,0,0)` and their right-side counterparts
`(n+2,s,0)`, `(n+1,s,0)`, `(n+1,0,0)`, where s=0.90 and n is the body length.
These are not prescribed graph vertices and all strands there have z=0.
The inspected `ensure_embedding` checks the pos/pts data contract; it does not
perform global segment-intersection checks.

The new exterior reverses the nesting assignments: top (1,3), middle (2,4),
bottom (3,5). Its same combinatorial edges are now disjoint away from their
legitimate common endpoints. The braid is built from monotone-x rational PL
crossing blocks. Adjacent lanes exchange with opposite nonzero heights; the
third lane stays outside their y-range. Distinct blocks have disjoint open
x-intervals. This supplies a direct nonintersection argument for every finite
word in the mathematical model. Nine representative rational PL graphs are
also checked segment-by-segment with exact predicates.

This is an explicit repaired realization. An ambient-isotopy claim from the
old self-intersecting object would be inappropriate. Nor does the theorem
prove numerical correctness of arbitrary-length floating-point notebook runs.

## 4. Identification with the archived Hankel representation

Let b_0,...,b_14 be the archived basis, with b_0 the empty word. Form

    C_j=M_(b_j) r,  O_i=ell M_(b_i).

The independently assembled matrices obey

    H=OC,  H_A=O M_A C,  H_B=O M_B C.

All three blocks match the archived file's Git blob identity. The determinant
of H evaluated at Y=2 is the same nonzero exact rational recorded by the audit.
Consequently H is invertible over Q(Y); both O and C are invertible. Hence

    T_a=H^(-1) H_a=C^(-1) M_a C,  a in {A,B}.

The archive uses `L=H[0,:]=ell C` and `R=e_0=C^(-1)r`. Therefore, for every word,

    L T_(w_1)...T_(w_m) R = ell M_(w_1)...M_(w_m) r = R_w.

This proves the all-word formula for the specified raw diagram family. The
infinite word-Hankel rank is at most 15 by the explicit realization and at
least 15 by the nonsingular finite block, so the representation is minimal.
This argument does not use agreement on longer words as a substitute for proof.

## 5. Completed verification

- The entire three-block Hankel file is reconstructed without reading training
  coefficients and matches its archived blob identity.
- Both exact pure-generator cubic identities hold:
  `(M-Y^2 I)(M-Y^(-2)I)(M-Y^(-4)I)=0`. The generators do not commute.
- 225 exact multiplication-matrix checks test all 3,375 basis associativity
  triples. Both crossing-inverse identities and the three-strand braid
  relation are checked symbolically, not at selected Y values.
- Five archived raw coefficient hashes match as explicit convention checks.
- 50 standalone pytest tests pass. Nine corrected rational PL graphs have no
  unintended contacts; nine old exterior control-polygon examples exhibit the
  six contacts described above.

The full production/native test suite was not rerun. The geometry audit is a
new exact control-polygon experiment, not a replay of the production PD
pipeline. Original model prompts, freezing chronology and missing historical
seed associations remain unrecovered. This is a computer-assisted algebraic
proof with inspectable code and exact arithmetic, not a proof-assistant
formalization of the implementation.
