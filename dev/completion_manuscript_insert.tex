\subsection{Full-grid spatial signatures and analytic continuation}
We retain the analytic source, its closed-voxel realization and its selected embedded graph as separate objects. The complete Hamiltonian scan contains 7,140 parameter points at $120^3$, with 60 equally spaced interpolation values including both endpoints, so that $\Delta\lambda=1/59$. The three TPMS scans contain all 1,323 original parameter points at the same voxel resolution. No small-region relabeling or abstract polynomial fallback is applied.

To distinguish source continuation from a selected-spine signature, we independently certify regular rectangles of the analytic parameter domain. A local critical-point calculation proves existence of events in some of the remaining rectangles. The additional level-resolved calculation fixes $c$ inside a queried rectangle and solves $(\nabla h_\lambda,h_\lambda-c)=0$ for $(x,y,z,\lambda)$. On the spherical boundary it solves $(\nabla h_\lambda-\mu\mathbf{x},|\mathbf{x}|^2-R^2,h_\lambda-c)=0$. \subsection{Closed-voxel local neighborhoods and source correspondence}
Each lattice vertex is assigned its eight-octant occupancy pattern. Its link is a triangular subcomplex of the octahedral sphere. A nonempty link must be a disk at a manifold boundary point or a sphere at an interior point.
